# Lazy Gamer store listing draft

## App name

Lazy Gamer

## Google Play short description

Grow a sleepy town, run shops, and crack clever puzzles. Play offline.

## App Store subtitle

Build a town. Crack puzzles.

## Long description

Welcome to Sleepy Hollow. Open a Coconut Stall, sell stock, and turn a small shop into a town full of roads, buildings and decorations. Stock grows while you’re away, so there’s something to collect when you come back.

Crack visual puzzles to speed up production and earn rewards. Stack crates, slice orders, balance scales and connect patterns. Pick from three difficulty bands or let the opening puzzles suggest a starting point. Try the daily challenge, discover new areas and dress up your town character and pet.

Play offline in the installed app, with no accounts, ads or purchases. Your town and progress stay on your device. Adjust music and sound effects separately, reduce motion or switch to easy-read text. A PIN-protected page lets grown-ups view and print a local progress summary.
